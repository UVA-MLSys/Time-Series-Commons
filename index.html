Mission Statement

"Time Series Commons" is a project advancing science and industry applications using time series. Our mission is to provide an AI agent that automatically recommends the best time series model for your data.

Explore Our Model Library

Discover our comprehensive collection of state-of-the-art time series models. From traditional statistical methods to cutting-edge deep learning architectures, find the perfect model for your forecasting needs.
